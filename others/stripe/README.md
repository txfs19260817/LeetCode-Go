## Hiring manager round


1. "Tell me about the most interesting project you have worked on and explain why you found it interesting."

Follow-up 1: "How did you implement [specific feature]?"

Follow-up 2: "Was this a course project for school?"

Follow-up 3: "How was it graded, and how many people were involved?"

2. "Have you ever encountered a problem that you were unable to solve on your own? How did you handle that situation?"

3. "Have you ever had to make a decision very quickly? How did you approach it?"

Follow-up: "What was the basis or rationale for that specific solution?"

4. "What are your future career plans? When looking for a job, what values do you prioritize the most?"

5. "How would you define success for yourself in your first six months after joining the team?"

6. "Please list the top three qualities or traits that you would bring to Stripe."
